Our Story

9 Years of Marble Mastery
from Kishangarh

"Where India's Marble Country Meets Industrial Precision"

Maharshi Marbles And Minerals was founded in Kishangarh — the marble capital of India — with a single mission: to bring industrial-grade marble powder and chips of uncompromising quality to businesses across the country.

Over nine years, we have grown from a local supplier to a trusted manufacturer with clients spanning the paint, construction, flooring, and chemical sectors.

Located in Kishangarh, Ajmer, Rajasthan — at the heart of India's marble belt — we have direct access to the finest white marble deposits, enabling us to control quality from source to supply.
